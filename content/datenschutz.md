## Cookies and consent

This website only uses cookies and tracking with your consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at
any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

**Consent management (Cookiebot).** To manage your consent we use Cookiebot by Usercentrics A/S,
Havnegade 39, 1058 Copenhagen, Denmark. Cookiebot stores your choice in the cookie “CookieConsent”
(up to 12 months) and logs the consent (time, choice, truncated IP address, browser details) so that we can
demonstrate it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (accountability) in conjunction with § 25(2) TDDDG.

**Performance measurement (AdPure, category Marketing).** With your consent we use AdPure by Rheinwork GmbH
to measure through which channels and ads visits and enquiries reach us. For this, the cookie “nx_vid”
(first-party cookie on rheinwork.com, up to 90 days) is set; we record entries (e.g. origin, campaign
parameters), contact clicks and form submissions – no form contents. Processing takes place on servers in
Germany. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG); without consent no measurement
takes place.

**Meta Pixel and Conversions API (category Marketing).** With your consent we use AdPure to integrate the
Meta Pixel of Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Meta"). This lets us measure
how well our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform and show ads to people who have visited our website. The
cookies "_fbp" and – if you arrive via a Meta ad – "_fbc" are set (up to 90 days each).

So that events still arrive when the pixel is blocked in the browser, AdPure additionally sends the same
events directly from our server to Meta (Conversions API). Meta merges both via a shared event ID and counts
them once. We transmit: the event (e.g. page view, click on phone or email, enquiry), the page visited without
parameters, time, IP address, browser identifier (user agent), the identifiers from "_fbp"/"_fbc", a hashed
identifier of your device (from the cookie "nx_vid") and the country. If you send us an enquiry, we report it
and later steps (e.g. qualified enquiry, appointment, order) to Meta; email address, phone number, name, country
and – if provided – postcode and city are only transmitted as a hash value (SHA-256). If you have already sent us
an enquiry, we also transmit the same hash values on later visits so that Meta can attribute the events to you.
We keep the IP address and browser identifier for this purpose for no more than 7 days.

We and Meta are joint controllers for collecting and transmitting this data to Meta (Art. 26 GDPR); the
agreement is available at [facebook.com/legal/controller_addendum](https://www.facebook.com/legal/controller_addendum).
Meta alone is responsible for any further processing; see
[facebook.com/privacy/policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy). Meta may transfer data to Meta
Platforms, Inc. in the USA, based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Commission's standard
contractual clauses. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG); without consent to
"Marketing" the pixel is not loaded and nothing is sent to Meta. Withdrawal takes effect for the future.

## Contact form

If you contact us via the contact form, we process the data you provide to
handle your request and any follow-up questions. We collect: first name, last name,
company (optional), email address, phone number (optional), the selected topic and your message. The fields
marked with an asterisk (*) are required in order to process your request. Your enquiry is stored in our own
customer management system (AdPure by Rheinwork GmbH). Only if you have consented to "Marketing" do we report
the enquiry to Meta as described under "Meta Pixel and Conversions API".

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to or performance of a contract) and,
for general enquiries, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your
request).

For the technical transmission and delivery we use the following processors:

- **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): receives the form submission via a serverless
  function and processes, among other data, your IP address. To protect against automated
  submissions (spam) we additionally use Cloudflare Turnstile.
- **Postmark** (ActiveCampaign, LLC, USA): sends your enquiry to us and a confirmation
  email to you.

Data processing agreements are in place with these providers. As both process data in
the USA, a transfer to a third country takes place, based on the EU Commission's
standard contractual clauses or certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Requests that lead to a contract or business correspondence are retained in line with
statutory retention periods (generally 6 to 10 years under the German Commercial Code
and Fiscal Code). All other request data is deleted no later than twelve months after
the request has been fully handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
